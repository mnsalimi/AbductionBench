\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Static delivery (21 datasets)}}\\
\midrule
\path{art} & SCS, MCS, Gen & Official alphaNLI test with public labels; alphaNLG test & Selection chooses a connecting event; generation writes one. The two source tasks are kept distinct. \\
\path{ecare} & SCS, MCS, Gen & Official dev: Causal Reasoning (cause questions) and Explanation Generation & Effect-prediction questions are excluded from selection; conceptual explanations form a separate generation task. \\
\path{climate_fever} & SCS, MCS & Released claim set, 1,535 items; no official split & Retrieved evidence is presented with each claim; all four verdict classes are retained. \\
\path{true_detective} & SCS, MCS & Whole release, 191 mysteries; no official split & Published answer options and gold labels are parsed into selection items. \\
\path{diagnosisarena} & SCS, MCS & Official test, 915 cases & The four released candidate diagnoses are used for the reported selection configuration. \\
\path{agentrx} & SCS, MCS & Root-cause-labelled Magentic-One and TAU trajectories, 97 cases; no official split & The failure summary is the default evidence; categories from the annotations form the candidate set. \\
\path{aer} & MCS & Official dev, 400 labelled items; test labels unavailable & Candidate causes are scored as sets; retrieved documents are supplied as titles and snippets. \\
\midrule
\path{aiops2025} (RCA100) & Gen & RCA100, 103 answer-keyed incidents & Alert text and bounded topology are shown; live telemetry queries are unavailable offline. \\
\path{commonwhy} & Gen & Head and Longtail pools, 7,430 questions each; no official split & Pools are combined for sampling; the answer-bearing rule field is withheld. \\
\path{crosstrace} & Gen & Released \path{test_ours.jsonl}, 107 items & The user turn supplies the observation; the source system prompt is not reused. \\
\path{enwn_entailmentbank} & Gen & Abductive-step data: EntailmentBank train (5,835) and ENWN validation (48) & Missing-premise steps are extracted; deductive and full-tree tasks are excluded. \\
\path{house_md} & Gen & Four released workbooks, 701 unique vignettes; no official split & Vignettes are deduplicated across workbooks and model-answer columns are excluded. \\
\path{hypogen} & Gen & Official test, 50 items & Title, abstract, and other answer-revealing fields are withheld. \\
\path{llm4biohypogen} & Gen & GPT-4 extraction, \path{test_unseen.json}, 197 studies & Only the unseen-study split is used; the hypothesis is withheld from the prompt. \\
\path{matter_to_mechanism} & Gen & Whole released table, 2,645 rows; no official split & Problem-side fields are shown; hypothesis, mechanism, outcome, and reasoning fields are withheld. \\
\path{medcasereasoning} & Gen & Official test, 897 case reports & Case evidence is presented for open-vocabulary diagnosis. \\
\path{medr_bench} & Gen & Diagnosis collection, 957 cases; no official split & Treatment cases are excluded; the concise diagnosis field supplies the reference. \\
\path{neulr} & Gen & Whole \path{abductive_neutral.json}, 1,000 items & The missing-fact task is retained; deductive and inductive files are excluded. \\
\path{proof_writer} & Gen & Official test, open-world depths 1, 2, 3, and 5 & Missing-fact abduction items are reconstructed from the released proofs. \\
\path{uncommonsense} & Gen & Official validation, 2,239 items; no test split & Human explanations provide references; model-written explanation fields are excluded. \\
\path{uniadilr_hgc} & Gen & Released \path{abduction.jsonl}, 500 items & The model predicts supporting statements, without being told the gold set size. \\
